DOES IT HOLD TOGETHER, AND THE FIRST TEN SECONDS
It is one made thing: one brass, one family of blue light, one typeface, the same panel and button everywhere, and
no seam where a scene was bolted on. Nothing stutters on this machine. The first ten seconds are the loader and the
arrival at Cellestial's star, and on a desktop they do say that whoever built this can build something
extraordinary: the writing in lightning is the real thing, and the star in its three brass rings is beautiful.
On a phone the loader is as good, but the picture it hands over to is small: Virgo is a band 147 px tall in the
middle of an 844 px screen, the star 83 px across, the empty stars 17 px (sky/open.jpg, stations-phone.jpg).
Where it looks unfinished: the meaning written over a constellation (grey, small, parked above the button); the
teleport flash (a different, heavier kind of line from everything else); the fall (one burst where all were asked
for); an empty station, where the star that should show brass traces is a plain dark bead.

RULINGS ON WHAT THE BUILDERS FLAGGED
1. One star bursts in the fall. CONFIRMED, and it is the worst thing found. The other eleven stars of Virgo (five of
   Libra) pass behind the soil's edge and are gone. The owner's words are "ALL the stars". Finding 1.
2. The flash's traces are whiter and thicker than the emblem's lines. CONFIRMED. 175 ms into a real teleport the
   bright traces are a median 5 px wide and their mean colour is #a4bcdc, a pale grey-blue covering 25% of the
   frame (numbers.txt; fx/flash-mid-desk.jpg). The emblem's lit lines are #5178c2, a saturated blue, a pixel or two
   wide at the size the loader shows them. At the peak the traces are white with a white bloom and the ground
   between them is lifted from black to slate blue (fx/flash-peak.jpg). The spec asks for "glowing --blue circuit
   lines ... on black" peaking to blue-white "at the centre". The thickness can be defended ("seen very close up");
   the colour and the lifted ground cannot. Finding 3.
3. At logo size the lit lines cannot be seen. CONFIRMED (chrome/logo.jpg): what is on screen is the unlit emblem
   with a blue glow on its node. It is 52 px wide; the fault is small but it is on every scene. Finding 9.
4. The empty star's brass traces cannot be seen at station size. CONFIRMED (sky/stars.jpg), and it matters more than
   the builders rate it: the empty star is the most repeated thing in the sky and it does not look like its asset.
   Its rim is not the "faint --blue outer glow at ~20%" of the spec either: sampled, the rim is #707a8c on average
   and #8f9cb5 at its brightest, a steel grey-blue at up to 70% brightness. Finding 4.
5. The meaning stands under its figure; nine placements never looked at. All twelve looked at at both sizes
   (sky/b7-all-desk.jpg, b7-all-phone.jpg, b7-all.txt). Nothing is clipped, nothing covers a star, a button, the
   logo or B11, at any figure. On the PHONE the placement is good: the words sit centred a line under the figure
   with room round them (ten figures 33 to 38 px under the lowest star; Libra and Cancer, the tall ones, 29 px
   above B11). On the DESKTOP it is poor: at ten figures the words stand at y 746 and Leo's two lines from y 698,
   all eleven ending 26 px above the exit button, wherever there is a gap between the lowest stars (x from 439 to
   738), so they read as a caption to the button; only Virgo's stand free (158 px above it). "Jesus is King." is
   the line nearest the button. And at both sizes the cooled letters are not brass. Finding 2.
6. Stars are small on the phone at wide figures. CONFIRMED: radius 8.6 px empty, 10.7 px lit (sky/stars.jpg); the
   six wide figures fill a band 147 to 214 px tall of the 844 (b7-all.txt). The tap targets are still 44 px.
   Finding 5.
7. The trunk of the mycelium is nearly white. PARTLY. Sampled on three rows under node 1 its brightest pixel is
   #d0e0f9 to #e8f6ff and only one or two pixels in a row have every channel over 200: a blue-white heart one to two
   pixels wide in a blue halo, the same language as the loader's lit wires. What does read wrong is its line: on
   the phone the trunk kinks and throws branches at near right angles, and while it grows it is thick and white,
   so it looks like lightning before it looks like mycelium (roots/descent-phone.jpg, grow-phone.jpg). Finding 10.

FINDINGS, WORST FIRST

1. BLOCKER — In the fall only one star crashes. Ranked a blocker because the owner asked for it in his own words
   ("ALL the stars ... crashing into the ground, sinking in, and exploding into the MYCELIUM") and the fall is the
   hinge of the site; by the letter of D2 ("A star hits the soil's top edge") it would be MAJOR.
   Evidence: transitions/fall-desk.jpg (470 ms: one burst; 627-911 ms: the rest pass the edge with nothing),
   fall-phone.jpg, walk/desk-08-fall.jpg, walk/phone-08-fall.jpg; logs/sky.log "onImpact fired 1 time(s)".
   Fix: give the sky's fall an impact per star (or have the director compute each star's crossing of surfaceY()
   from its fall curve) and fire fx.impact(x, y) for each, smaller and shorter for all but the first, staggered as
   they land; keep one roots.growFrom from the first. fx/impact-desk.jpg shows the burst works at any x. While
   there: keep an impact's x at least 15% in from a screen edge (phone-08: cut off at the left edge), and start
   the view's descent as the last star lands so the half-second of black from +1000 to +1500 ms goes.

2. MAJOR — B7, the meaning: the cooled words are not brass, and on the desktop they are a small caption over the
   exit button. Evidence: b7-all.txt (mean colour of the letters #95888c desktop, #97898b phone, at every figure;
   "studio" in the same kind of screenshot is #a38264, computed #ae8a68); sky/meaning-settled.jpg,
   meaning-still.jpg; sky/b7-all-desk.jpg (eleven of twelve ending 26 px above B11; "the Lamb" is 165x44 px on a
   1440 px screen).
   Fix: cool the letters to --color-brass-text with the A4 letter's lighting (the token "studio" uses) and keep the
   thin blue line; on screens 1024 px and wider set the line larger (48-56 px) and place it by the figure, in the
   largest free area inside or beside the figure's own bounds, before falling back to the band above B11; give

By the owner's definition (a scene is done when it matches its reference, not when it renders): LOADING is done for
A1 to A4, at both sizes, and is the best work here; its last second, A5, is not, because the star's pieces do not

written in grey and on the desktop sits as a caption over the button, and on a phone the wide figures and their

soil, nodes, tiles, the network and the return button match their assets and the spec, with only polish left on
the guided light and the trunk's line. THE CLOSE is done. THE CHROME is done for the menu and its panel, nearly for
the logo (the idle state is nearer unlit), and not for the teleport flash, which is the wrong light. THE WARP is
done. Nothing breaks a rule on assets, the colour of the ground, sound, social icons or copy; the one blocker and
five majors above are what stand between this build and the owner's bar.
