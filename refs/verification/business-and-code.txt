====================================================================================

Branch redesign-build, site code as at c345872 (a99c4e3 on top of it changes PROGRESS.md
only). Inspected 20-21 Nov 2026. Baseline: a31d2ee ("spec freeze"), which is byte-identical
to orgin/main, the live site, on every protected path (git diff orgin/main a31d2ee over
workers, src, public, wrangler.jsonc prints nothing).

No file of the site was changed. Everything here was run by me; nothing is taken from
PROGRESS.md. Evidence is beside this file in refs/verification/business-and-code/:
  *.log, *.txt        the output of every run quoted below
  suites/             the builders' check suites as I ran them (logs and their outputs)
  tools/              the scripts I wrote (each says in its header how to run it)
  shots/, sheet-*.jpg screenshots (written as PNG by the scripts, kept as JPEG, 1600 px at most)

Browser runs: the builders' suites against the dev server; everything of mine against the
PRODUCTION build (pnpm build with the Turnstile test key, pnpm astro preview --port 4611),
system Chromium through playwright-core, real GPU (NVIDIA through ANGLE) unless stated,
at 1440x900 and 390x844 (scale 2, mobile, touch).

SHORT ANSWER
  The business is not broken. The three protected systems are untouched in logic, the form
  posts exactly what it posted before, /admin loads, /start links resolve, and the claim rule
  holds end to end. My own checks on the production build: the claim rule 64 of 64, /admin
  and /start 29 of 29, the degraded states 101 of 101, the form 121 of 126 (the five are one
  opening in which Cloudflare's widget did not arrive; 15 of 15 when repeated).
  Thirteen findings in the table at the end; three of them matter:
    1. the browser-tab icon is the Astro framework's logo (an image that is not from the pack);
    2. the loader runs over 8 s on anything slower than a fast desktop, and the builders' own
       loader check fails with everything in (8.20 s);
    3. a claimed star is lit only in the claimer's own browser, and for 47 of the 146 stars
       the owner's email does not say which star was claimed.

====================================================================================
VERDICT
====================================================================================
The owner's hardest requirement is met: the redesign sits on top of the business and did not
reach into it. The three protected systems are byte-identical in logic, the form posts the
same five fields and not one character more, a star is claimed by a 200 and by nothing else,
/admin and /start work, and the page degrades to readable offers without WebGL, without
script and under reduced motion; the code is careful and I could not make the director lose
its place. It is not yet done by his own tests. One rule is broken as written, and trivially
mended: the tab icon is a stock logo from outside the pack. One of his measurable criteria
fails on real devices: the loader is built with a tenth of a second to spare under 8 s and
runs 8.2 to 10 s on anything slower, and the builders' own check says so. And the promise
behind the sky is thinner than it reads: a claimed star is lit in one browser only, and for a
third of the stars the owner is not even told which was claimed, so "the lit stars are the
work" will show every new visitor an empty sky until he decides how stars become shared.
Fix the icon and the loader's timing, put the star question (findings 3 and 4) in front of
the owner as the decision it is, and the business-and-code side of this site is ready.
