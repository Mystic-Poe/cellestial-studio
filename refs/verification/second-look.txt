    ground is in the lower 40% of the frame from +1.1 s and keeps climbing).
  WHAT IS LEFT
  a. A HARD CUT at about +1.0 to +1.15 s. The web of root-light that the bursts threw into the ground, a third of
     the screen wide and the brightest thing on screen, vanishes in ONE frame. desk-virgo-cut.jpg: +1123 ms mean
     light 19.2, +1134 ms 11.6 (40% of the screen's light gone in 11 ms; 23.3 at +1103). Sagittarius 21.8 -> 15.6
     in one frame; phone Virgo 21.5 -> 16.8. Cause, read in src/experience/fx/layer.ts: IMPACTS_MAX = 18, "past
     this many at once the oldest, which is all but spent, makes way". It is not spent: the far stars' forty small
     impacts all land within 150 ms of each other at the centre and push every large burst out while it is 270
     to 650 ms into its 760 ms. So the crash does not grow into the mycelium: it is switched off, and one trunk is left.
     Fix: do not shift a live burst out. Either merge the far stars' impacts into a few (one per 100 ms at the
     centre), or drop the newest small one when the list is full, or raise the cap and draw the small ones as a
     spot only (no rays, no sparks), so the large bursts live out their 760 ms and fade.
  b. A flat white lozenge lies on the ground beside the head of the trunk from about +1.0 s to +1.8 s (those same
     forty small impacts, all at the screen's centre, drawn on top of each other): desk-virgo-whole.jpg +1200 to
     +1800, desk-aries-dense.jpg +966 on. It reads as a white smear, not as stars landing. Fix: spread the far
     stars' landings across the width they fall over, and cap the summed light so it cannot clip to flat white.
  c. At Sagittarius on the desktop the 25 bursts land within 350 px of each other and add up to one solid white
     mass 250 px wide for 0.25 s (desk-sagittarius-dense.jpg +672 to +840). Same fix: cap the sum.

     nothing recording: average 19 to 20 ms, 2 to 4 frames over 34 ms, worst 67 to 83 ms; the sky's governor has
