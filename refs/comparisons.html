Where it stands

  • Built and live. The loader, the sky, the fall, the Roots, the close and the logo and menu, on top of the contact form, /admin and /start.
  • Inspected five times by inspectors who built none of it. The last full look ruled every scene done at both sizes, with no blocker and nothing major. The fifth, a narrow look at the "claim yours" label, ruled the same and left three minors in the tightest knots of stars.
  • Changed the same afternoon, on the owner's word: the sky is shared (a claimed star is lit for every visitor; its text only after he approves it), a second tap sends more beads, no two stars touch, and stars 100 to 146 are named in his email.

Waiting on the owner

  • One real message through the live form. It needs a person past the spam check, and it is also the first real test of the shared sky and of the approve link in the email.
  • The logo and menu are hidden while the loader plays. The brief says "present throughout".
  • Three minors from the fifth look, all about where "claim yours" stands in the tightest knots. Not looked at again since the stars there were drawn smaller and apart.

Loading

It has to feel like the reference video in the asset pack: the C lights, the bolt strikes, the wordmark is written and cools to brass. 6 to 8 seconds, never under 6.

The Sky

Twelve constellations in the spec's order, each the real star pattern, on a faint brass honeycomb. The sky starts empty except for Cellestial's own star.

The fall

The owner's words: all the stars fall out of the sky, speeding faster and faster, crashing into the ground, sinking in, and exploding into the mycelium.

The Roots

Five nodes in the spec's order, one tile open at a time, the prices inside the tiles, the wording character for character as the addendum has it.

Close and chrome

"Claim your star" big, "Contact me" smaller under it. The logo and the three-bar menu throughout; the menu teleports to four places.

Live, 23 November

What cellestialstudio.com served after publishing, and the two things the owner asked for that afternoon.

Reduced motion

A still sky with the exit button already there. No flight, no loader.
