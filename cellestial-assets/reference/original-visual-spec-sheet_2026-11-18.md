## HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

- Work top to bottom, ONE element at a time.
- For each element marked **TO GENERATE**, write ONE unique image prompt for it, using its Appearance + Purpose below.
- Gabriel runs the prompt in Gemini, iterates until he loves the result, then moves to the next element.
- Elements marked **✅ LOCKED** are done. Do NOT write prompts for them. Use them as the style reference for everything else.
- **Style rule:** Every new element must look like it belongs in the same world as the locked pieces in Section A — same brass material, same blue circuitry, same black ground — unless the element's own description says otherwise.
- **Lock rule:** Nothing is locked until Gabriel sees a generated result and says yes to it. Do not invent extra visual details (extra colours, fonts, textures, objects) that are not in the element's description.
- **Plumbing note (not visual, do not prompt for it):** This is a visual redesign only. The contact form, /admin editor, and /start links already work and are not part of this.

## SECTION B — THE SKY (Hero)

Setting for all B prompts unless stated: pure black deep-space sky, endless 3D depth, no text or copy anywhere except where an element says so. Silent, still, wondrous feel.

**B1 + B2. Sky background with brass honeycomb — TO GENERATE (one image, they are never seen apart)**
- Appearance: Deep black endless sky/space with real 3D depth. Floating through it, a very faint brass honeycomb — a hexagonal grid made of thin brass lines, barely visible, stretching away in 3D perspective. No stars lit yet, no text.
- Purpose: The empty world the visitor first flies into. The honeycomb is the hidden structure that holds the stars up.

**B3. Epicentre site star — TO GENERATE (form corrected by Gabriel, Nov 18, chat #4)**
- Appearance: A glowing radiant star CORE at the centre, surrounded by spinning RINGS made of shattered brass pieces — the broken-apart remains of the little brass star from A5. The brass ring pieces are cracked, with blue electric light and blue circuits glowing through the cracks and running along the pieces. The rings spin/orbit around the radiant core at angles, like an armillary/orrery. This sits at the centre of a faint constellation pattern, clearly the most important light in the frame. Faint empty star points around it form the rest of its pattern.
- Purpose: Cellestial's own star. The centre of the first pattern and the start of the flight.

**B4. Empty star — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A single small, dim, unlit star point — a faint node of light that looks waiting/switched off, sitting on a constellation line, black sky behind.
- Purpose: A spot a visitor can claim for their own site. Must read clearly as "empty / available" next to B5.

**B5. Lit / claimed star — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: The same star point as B4, now fully lit — bright, steady, warm glow, clearly switched on and permanent. Same size and position style as B4 so the pair reads as before/after.
- Purpose: A claimed spot — someone's site lives here. Must read clearly as "taken / alive" next to B4.

**B6. Constellation pattern — TO GENERATE (one pattern image as the system example)**
- Appearance: A group of star points (mix of B4 empty and a few B5 lit) joined in the real shape of a named constellation, floating as one flat-ish layer in the black sky, faint brass honeycomb behind it. No text in the image.
- Purpose: One layer of the flight. There are 12, flown through in this order: Virgo, Libra, Scorpius, Sagittarius, Capricornus, Aquarius, Pisces, Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo. Generate ONE as the style example — the rest follow its style in the build.

**B7. Meaning reveal text — TO GENERATE (style frame only)**
- Appearance: A short line of words appearing in the black sky as if written by lightning — letters formed by jagged electric-blue light, mid-cooling toward brass with blue circuitry, like A3 settling into A4's material. Black background, nothing else.
- Purpose: When every star in a constellation is claimed, its meaning is revealed written in light this way. After the last one (Leo), the line reads: "Jesus is King."

**B8. Beads of light — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A trail of small round blue beads of light flowing along the faint brass honeycomb lines, curving with the grid, fading at the tail. Black sky, close view.
- Purpose: What happens when a visitor touches/clicks — light flows from their touch along the honeycomb toward one empty star.

**B9. "claim yours" pulse — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: One empty star (B4) glowing slightly brighter, with the small words "claim yours" in tiny glowing brass letters directly underneath it, on black sky. Nothing else in frame.
- Purpose: Marks the star the beads of light (B8) landed on — this one can be claimed.

**B10. Empty-star preview panel — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A small, clean floating panel in the sky — dark black face, thin brass-circuit outline, a few short lines of plain white text inside with one or two brass words. Simple, readable, not busy.
- Purpose: Clicking an empty star opens this — a short breakdown of what Cellestial can build (Site / Site + Systems / Site + App / Add-ons / Care).

**B11. Exit button "See what holds up the sky" — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A single button, brass circuitry style matching A1/A4 — brass form, thin blue circuit line, round nodes — with the words "See what holds up the sky" on it, centred on a black background.
- Purpose: Sits at the bottom centre of the screen after one full flight through all 12 constellations. Clicking it ends the sky scene.

## SECTION E — ROOTS ("What we build")

Setting for all E prompts: underground, dark earth/soil, still and grounded — the opposite feel of the sky. Plain and readable. This is where prices and offers are explained, so clarity beats spectacle.

**E1 + E2. Earthen background with mycelium network — TO GENERATE (one image, never seen apart)**
- Appearance: Dark underground soil/earth filling the frame. Running through it, a glowing blue mycelium network — branching root-like lines, thinner branches off thicker ones, small glowing nodes where they join, spreading across the whole frame like roots.
- Purpose: The ground the whole scene lives in. The network carries light from offer to offer.

**E3. Guided light pulse — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A close view of one branch of the E2 mycelium with one section of it lit much brighter — a pulse of blue light part-way along the branch, heading toward a node.
- Purpose: Shows the visitor where to go next. It suggests, it does not force.

**E4. Node — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: One larger glowing knot/node on the mycelium network, brighter than the lines around it, clearly clickable — like a bulb growing on a root. Dark earth behind.
- Purpose: One offer lives here. There are 5 in a descending line, each deeper than the last: 1 Site · 2 Site + Systems · 3 Site + App · 4 Add-ons (AI + automation) · 5 Care. Generate ONE as the style example.

**E5. Panel / tile — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: A dark black rectangular panel with a brass-circuit outline (same brass + thin blue circuit as A1), floating over the dark earth/mycelium background. Inside: short lines of plain white text, with the important words in brass. Clean, roomy, easy to read. (Placeholder text is fine — real wording is already written and locked separately.)
- Purpose: Clicking a node (E4) opens this panel with that offer's details and price inside.

**E6. Return-to-sky button — TO GENERATE**
- Appearance: Same brass circuitry button style as B11, on the dark earth background, with the idea of upward motion in its styling. No words needed in the image unless they come naturally — its job is clear from placement at the top of the ground.
- Purpose: At the top of the Roots scene — clicking it warp-launches the visitor back up into the sky.
